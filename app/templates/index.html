Welcome to TrafficWatch

  1. Local only. TrafficWatch binds 127.0.0.1 — your data stays on this PC.
  2. Day / night globe. Use Night, Day-night, or Day under Settings; labels stay off for a clean map.
  3. Alerts & Status. Actionable intel hits land under Alerts; Status holds conn counts and system chips. Live stays the full list.
  4. Inspect & Block. Open an alert or Live row for detail; Block is one-click from the known IP/process. Typed confirm remains for Kill only.
  5. Baseline learning. Per-program baseline learns ~7 days (and enough samples), then ready — departures and exfil-ish show as alerts.
  6. Direction filter. All / Out / In / Listen; inbound from public IPs is highlighted.
